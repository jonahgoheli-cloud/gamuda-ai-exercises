Air Fryer Fried Chicken

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 12 minutes

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • 6 chicken leg quarters
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 cups of buttermilk

For the coating

  • 1.5 cup of all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon season salt (such as Himalayan sea salt)
  • 1 teaspoon of kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon of ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon of garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon of tumeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon of paprika
  • Spray olive oil for cooking

Directions

Step 1

Marinate the chicken:

Season the chicken well with salt and pepper and then add it to a bowl with buttermilk. Chicken pieces should be covered with buttermilk. Let it sit for at least an hour or up to overnight.

Step 2

Bread the chicken:

In a shallow dish, whisk together 1.5 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tbsp seasoned salt, 1 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp ground black pepper, 1 tbsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp turmeric powder, and 1 tbsp paprika. Lift each chicken quarter from the buttermilk, drain off excess liquid, and thoroughly coat in the flour mixture.

Step 3

Air-fry the chicken:

Preheat your air fryer to 380°F (193°C) and spray the basket with olive oil spray. Arrange the chicken in a single layer and spray the tops liberally with olive oil spray to cover any dry flour spots. Air fry for 15 minutes, flip, spray the other side, and cook for another 10–12 minutes. Increase the temperature to 400°F (204°C) for the last 3–5 minutes until extra crispy and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).

Step 4

Serve:

Transfer the chicken to a wire rack or serving plate and let it rest for 5 minutes so the juices redistribute and the crust stays crisp.

Step 5

Enjoy!

Difficulty: Delicious
